Return and Refund Policy

Last updated: May 09, 2025

Thank you for choosing to stay with Daun Retreats. We value your experience and aim to provide clear guidelines on our refund and cancellation process.

Booking & Payment Terms

Deposit Refund

  • • A security deposit is collected before check-in.
  • • The deposit will be refunded within 5–7 working days after check-out, provided there are no damages or violations of our villa policies.
  • • The refund will be made using the same payment method used during booking, unless otherwise requested.

Cancellation Policy

  • • Any cancellation must be made at least 4 weeks (28 days) before the scheduled check-in date to qualify for a refund of the deposit.
  • • Cancellations made within 4 weeks of the arrival date will result in the forfeiture of the deposit.

No Refund After Full Payment

  • ! Once full payment has been made for the booking, no refund will be issued under any circumstances.
  • • This includes cancellations, no-shows, or changes in stay duration.

Damages and Deductions

• Guests are fully responsible for any damages caused to the villa or its contents during their stay.
• Daun Retreats reserves the right to deduct repair or replacement costs from the security deposit based on the actual value of damaged or missing items.
• In cases where damages exceed the deposit amount, guests may be required to pay the balance.
